Privacy at BETTER

BETTER stores your profile, goals, tasks, preferences and other app data locally in your browser. If you create and use a BETTER account, signed-in data is also synchronized to the connected Supabase service so you can continue on another device.

Accounts and cloud data

Account authentication is handled by Supabase. BETTER does not receive or store your account password. Signed-in BETTER profile and progress data are stored in the BETTER cloud tables associated with your account. Access is restricted so users can access only their own records.

Your control

You can export your BETTER data from the Profile page and delete local browser data there. If you have an account, you can also use Delete account to request deletion of the account and the BETTER cloud data associated with it.

Research and recommendations

The current MVP does not claim to perform live web research or access live product databases. Demo product information is labeled as demo content.

Analytics and third parties

The app may load the Supabase JavaScript client and Vercel web analytics used by the deployment. BETTER does not currently connect live shopping, retailer, product-review or research databases.

Changes

This notice may be updated as BETTER evolves. The current version describes the public beta and is not legal advice.

For a commercial launch, have this notice and the app's data practices reviewed by qualified legal counsel.
