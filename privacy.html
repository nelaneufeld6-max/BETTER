Privacy at BETTER

BETTER MVP stores your profile, goals, tasks and other personal data locally in your browser. The current public MVP does not require an account or send that personal profile to a BETTER database.

Your control

You can delete local BETTER data from the Profile page. Clearing your browser's site data also removes the local profile.

Research and recommendations

The current MVP does not claim to perform live web research or access live product databases. Demo product information is labeled as demo content.

Future services

Authentication, cloud synchronization, analytics and other services may be introduced in a future version. Their collection and use will be documented before they are enabled.

This is an MVP privacy notice, not legal advice. Update it with counsel before commercial launch.
